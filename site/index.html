    How the number works

    • Everyone starts at 1500.
    • Early wars swing hard. After many wars, the number sticks.
    • A CW is four sets, each first to 3 (max 12–0). W–L–D only cares who scored more. Rating also uses the gap.

    How to read the board

    Rating
    Strength from the published war history.
    Uncertainty
    Low = trusted. High = still a guess. Not round difference.
    Avg opp
    Schedule, using opponents’ ratings today.
    Win%
    Ignores who you played. Rating and Avg opp do not.

    Use Established (15+ wars, Uncertainty under 100) if you want the table that has settled. Short unbeaten runs with high Uncertainty are noise.
