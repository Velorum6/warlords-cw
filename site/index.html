Not official. Avg opp is the mean of opponents’ current Rating (one count per war), not the rating on the night you played them. Win% ignores schedule; Rating and Avg opp do not. Score margin does not affect Rating: 12–3 and 9–7 are the same win. Draws count as 0.5.

How the number works

Everyone starts at 1500. Early wars move the number a lot; after many wars it sticks.

Opponent strength is in the update. Beating a stronger clan is worth more than beating a weaker one. The round score only decides win, loss, or draw.

How to read the board

  • Rating — strength estimate from the war history.
  • Uncertainty — HIGH = guess, LOW = trusted.
  • Avg opp — schedule column: who you have been playing, measured with today’s ratings.
  • A high win% on a soft schedule is not the same as a high win% on a hard one. That is why a clan can farm wars and still sit mid-table, while another with a similar win% sits at the top.
  • Short unbeaten runs with high Uncertainty are noise. Switch to Established if you want the table that has actually settled.
